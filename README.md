## 🔬 Research

I work on the **reliability of audio deepfake detection** — making detectors robust to adversarial
perturbations, and understanding why they fail to generalise across languages and unseen speech
generators. I also work on **biomedical image analysis** at the Big-Matrix R&D Lab.

- 🎧 **Primary:** audio deepfake detection, adversarial training, cross-lingual speech
- 🧬 **Also:** medical image segmentation, multimodal AI for healthcare
- 🎓 **Currently seeking Master's and PhD positions**
- 📫 Reach me at **ifti3061@gmail.com**

### 📄 Publications

**[C1]** <ins>Iftikhar Ahmed</ins>, Mueid Islam Arian, Nusrat Jahan Lisa.
*"Mel-Spectrogram Representations for CNN Based Adversarial Defense for Audio Deepfake Detection."*
**ICMLT 2026**, Germany.
[![DOI](https://img.shields.io/badge/DOI-10.1109%2FICMLT69916.2026.11689539-00629B?style=flat-square)](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICMLT69916.2026.11689539)

> Mel-spectrograms beat LFCC for CNN-based detection — **0.034 vs 0.053 EER (36% better)**,
> outperforming GMM baselines and RawNet2. Adaptive adversarial training cuts EER under attack
> from **0.70 → 0.107** (FGSM) and **0.90 → 0.154** (PGD).

**[S1]** <ins>Iftikhar Ahmed</ins>, M. N. Mithila, A. Dhrubo, M. A. Qayum, M. Khan.
*"DASF-Net: Dual-Adversarial Spectral Fusion for Cross-Lingual and Adversarially Robust Audio Deepfake Detection."*
